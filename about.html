ABOUT LEONIDA INSIDER

An independent companion for curious fans.

Leonida Insider is a free Android companion for people following GTA VI. The current app preview brings together original editorial reading, source links, article search, local saved articles, and a link to our Discord community. The iOS app is under review.

How we approach coverage

We link to original publisher and platform sources wherever possible. We distinguish confirmed announcements from commentary and fan theories. Articles prepared with AI assistance are drafts until reviewed; AI output alone is not verification.

Current and planned features

News, search, local saved reading and community access are shown in the latest app preview. Map and location guides, Easter eggs and cheat code guides are planned additions. They are not included in the current release.

Corrections and support

If a statement needs correcting, email app support with the page link, the statement, and a primary source where available. This is also the support address listed in the app’s terms.

Independent and unofficial

Leonida Insider is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. We do not provide GTA VI, early access, or publisher accounts.
