Companion app privacy

Leonida Insider • Updated 6 November 2026

You can read without signing in. Optional Google or Apple sign-in provides an account profile. We receive your provider identifier, name and email when the provider supplies them, and your Google profile image if available. Apple lets you choose a private relay email.

Our server verifies your sign-in and stores your account identifier, name, email, optional profile image URL, account creation time and latest sign-in time. We use these details to provide and maintain your account. We do not receive your Google or Apple password. Provider credentials are used to verify sign-in and, where necessary, revoke access on deletion.

Bookmarks and cached reading are stored on your device. They are not currently synced across devices. Signing out clears the local account session.

To delete a signed-in account, choose Delete account in the account screen and sign in again to confirm ownership. We confirm completion only after the server deletes your profile. Apple access is revoked as part of that process. If the server or Apple access revocation cannot complete, the account is not deleted and the app shows an error so you can retry. If your profile has been deleted but removing the Google connection fails, the app tells you the account is deleted and offers a connection-removal retry or asks you to manage access in your Google account.

Google and Apple process sign-in under their own privacy policies. The API backend is hosted on Render, and account records are stored in a Neon PostgreSQL database. Render and Neon process the information needed to host and maintain these services. Loading a Google profile image makes a request to Google's image service. Opening a source, YouTube or Discord link opens an external service with its own privacy practices. The app does not automatically send your account details to Discord; Discord is opened only when you choose the community link.

We do not sell your account details or use this sign-in to collect your contacts or social graph. No advertising tracking or additional analytics service is enabled by this notice. Network hosting services may process connection information needed to deliver requests. The app does not offer wallpaper uploads or downloads. Remote push alerts are unavailable in this version, and automatic push registration is disabled.

Privacy contact: cybernerd404notfound@gmail.com (the support email published on the app's Google Play listing).
