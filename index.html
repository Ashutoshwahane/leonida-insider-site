04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
come along.

Here’s what to expect.

What is Leonida Insider?

An independent companion for people following GTA VI. Read sourced editorial notes, search reviewed articles, and keep useful stories saved on your device.

Is this the GTA VI game or an official app?

No. Leonida Insider does not download or run the game, provide early access, or unlock game content. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Is the app free?

The app is free to download. Advertising and an optional monthly subscription to remove ads are planned. Subscription pricing and availability have not been announced.

When can I get it on iPhone?

The iOS app is currently under App Store review. We will add the download link after approval. There is no confirmed availability date yet.

Do I need to create an account?

You can browse as a guest. Signing in is optional.

Does it include cheat codes or hidden locations?

There is no verified cheat code or hidden location guide collection in the current release candidate. We want future guides to be useful and checked before we publish them.

Will my saved articles sync across devices?

Saved articles are stored on your device. They do not currently sync across devices.

How do you choose what to publish?

We focus on original editorial context with links to primary sources. Commentary and speculation should be clearly distinguished from official announcements.
